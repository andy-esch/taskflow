# Vegetarian pate: mushroom, walnut, and lentil

## Objective

Build a spreadable stand-in for pork liver pate from finely minced mushroom
duxelles (cremini, cooked dry until all moisture's gone), toasted walnuts
ground fine, and cooked red lentils for body, bound with butter and a hit of
brandy and thyme for the gamey depth pate usually gets from liver. The
duxelles needs to cook well past the point where it looks done — any
residual water turns the final puree grainy instead of smooth.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Mushroom duxelles cooked until visibly dry, no released liquid left
- [x] Walnuts toasted before grinding, not raw
- [x] Texture blended smooth, not chunky — passes a spreadability test on bread
- [x] Brandy and thyme balanced so neither dominates

## Out of scope

- A liver-mimicking mock pate using nutritional yeast tricks — going for its
  own flavor identity instead of imitation.
