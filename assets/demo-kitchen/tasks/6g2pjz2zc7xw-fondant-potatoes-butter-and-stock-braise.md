# Fondant potatoes: butter-and-stock braise

## Objective

Cut russets into thick pucks, trim the edges square, sear both flat faces
hard in butter until deep golden, then add stock halfway up the sides with
garlic and thyme and braise (stovetop or 375°F oven) until a knife meets no
resistance and the stock has mostly reduced into a glaze. The center should
turn custardy while the seared faces stay intact — the braise liquid level
matters more than people expect, since too much drowns the sear and too
little scorches the pan dry before the centers finish.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Pucks cut and trimmed to an even thickness
- [ ] Sear achieves deep, even gold on both flat faces before liquid goes in
- [ ] Braise liquid held at roughly halfway up the puck sides
- [ ] Centers custardy on knife test, stock reduced to a glaze by the end
