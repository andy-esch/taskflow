# Lemongrass-marinated tofu, grilled and sliced

## Objective

Press extra-firm tofu for 30 minutes to force out water, then marinate in
minced lemongrass, garlic, shallot, soy sauce, and a little sugar for at
least an hour so the marinade actually penetrates past the surface. Grill or
sear hard enough to get real char on the outside before slicing into
sandwich-width slabs — the char is doing the flavor work the classic pork
version gets from fat rendering, so a gentle pan-fry undersells it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Tofu pressed a full 30 minutes before marinating
- [ ] Marinade time at least 1 hour, ideally overnight
- [ ] Visible char achieved on both sides before slicing
- [ ] Slabs cut to a width that layers cleanly in the roll

## Out of scope

- Seitan version — tofu first, seitan as a later variant if this doesn't land.
