# Build dal makhani: black urad + rajma, overnight soak, slow simmer

## Objective

Whole black urad and a handful of rajma, soaked 8+ hours, then simmered low
for a genuine 4-6 hours — not the 45-minute pressure-cooker shortcut — until
the lentils break down into the dish's signature velvety body. Butter and
cream go in during the last 20 minutes, not the first, so they don't split
over the long cook. The color should darken from gray-brown toward
near-black as the lentils release starch and the tomato reduces in.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Urad + rajma soaked minimum 8 hours before cooking
- [ ] Simmer held at a bare bubble for 4+ hours, stirred occasionally so it
      doesn't catch on the bottom
- [ ] Butter and cream folded in during the final 20 minutes only
- [ ] Texture check: lentils fully collapsed, no separate grains visible

## Out of scope

- Smoking the dal with a live coal (dhungar) — a separate finishing technique.
