# Waffle-cut potato chips on the mandoline

## Objective

Attempted waffle-cut (crinkle-lattice) chips using the mandoline's crimping
blade, rotating the potato 90 degrees between passes. Shelved — the crimping
blade chewed and tore the slices instead of cutting them cleanly, and the
uneven thickness across each lattice cut meant the fry never colored evenly.
Not worth further time without a purpose-built waffle-cut tool.
