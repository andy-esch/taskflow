# Vegetarian duck-fat-style roast potatoes

## Objective

Duck fat's savoriness in classic roast potatoes comes largely from rendered
meat proteins browning at high heat, which a plain vegetable oil can't fake
directly. The plan is to steep potato peels, a smashed garlic clove, and
rosemary in refined coconut oil (chosen for its high smoke point and neutral
base) over low heat for 20 minutes to pull out some of that toasty,
savory depth before roasting the par-boiled potatoes in the strained fat at
450°F. Parked behind the drivetrain of higher-priority tasks — worth trying
but not urgent.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Peel-and-aromatics steep tested for flavor transfer into the oil
- [ ] Strained fat used to roast a par-boiled batch at 450°F
- [ ] Blind taste comparison against a plain-oil batch
- [ ] Verdict recorded either way

## Out of scope

- Actual animal-fat rendering — the whole point is a vegetarian stand-in.
