# Pressure cooker vs open pot for toor dal texture

## Objective

Cook two identical batches of toor dal side by side — one at 3 whistles in a
stovetop pressure cooker (roughly 12-15 minutes at pressure), one at a bare
simmer in an open pot for 45 minutes — and compare texture, starch release,
and how much water each needs topped up. The pressure cooker wins on time;
the question is whether the open pot's slower breakdown gives a
noticeably creamier result worth the extra 30 minutes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Both batches started from the same soak time and water ratio
- [x] Whistle count and open-pot time both logged
- [x] Side-by-side texture and starch-release comparison done
- [x] Verdict recorded: pressure cooker for weeknights, open pot when there's time

## Out of scope

- Instant Pot electric pressure cooking — stovetop only for this comparison.
