# Coconut-milk dal riff for a potluck

## Objective

A coconut-milk-finished dal was floated as a potluck option, using moong dal
simmered with coconut milk in the last 10 minutes instead of ghee and cream.
Shelved after a blind taste test against the standard tadka masoor dal — the
coconut milk muted the spice bloom and read as a different, less interesting
dish. Not pursuing further unless the group specifically asks for a
dairy-free option again.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Coconut-milk version cooked and tasted against the control
- [x] Verdict: shelved, tadka masoor wins on flavor clarity
