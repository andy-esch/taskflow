# Chana dal with amchur and asafoetida

## Objective

Chana dal should hold its shape rather than collapse into mush like toor or
masoor — soak 1 hour, then simmer just until tender-firm, testing every few
minutes past the 20-minute mark. A pinch of asafoetida in the tadka oil and
a finishing dust of amchur (dried mango powder) give it a sharper, more
citrus-forward tang than a squeeze of lemon does.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Chana dal soaked 1 hour minimum before cooking
- [ ] Grains intact and distinct at serving, not broken down
- [ ] Asafoetida bloomed in the tempering oil, not added raw
- [ ] Amchur added off heat so its tang doesn't cook off

## Out of scope

- Chana dal ground into vada or fritter batter — that's a different dish.
