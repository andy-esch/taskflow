# Do chua: pickled daikon and carrot ratio

## Objective

Julienne daikon and carrot to matchstick width, salt them and let them sit
15 minutes to draw out water and firm the texture, then rinse and pack into
a brine of roughly 1:1:4 vinegar:sugar:water with a pinch of extra salt. The
salt-draw step before pickling is what keeps the sticks crunchy at day 3
instead of going limp — skipping it lets the vegetables absorb too much
liquid too fast.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Daikon and carrot cut to consistent matchstick width
- [x] Pre-salt and rinse step done before brining, not skipped
- [x] Brine ratio landed on and written down for repeatability
- [x] Crunch test at day 3 — still snappy, not soft

## Out of scope

- Fermented (lacto-pickled) daikon — this is the quick vinegar version only.
