# Sriracha mayo from scratch

## Objective

Emulsify a base mayo from egg yolk, neutral oil added in a slow stream, and a
splash of rice vinegar, then cut it with sriracha and a squeeze of lime for
the spread layer that goes on both cut sides of the roll. The oil has to go
in genuinely slowly at the start — a food processor still breaks if the
first quarter-cup is dumped in rather than drizzled, and there's no fixing a
broken emulsion after the fact except starting over with a fresh yolk.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Emulsion holds through the full oil addition, no breaking
- [x] Sriracha and lime balanced so the mayo reads spicy-bright, not just hot
- [x] Consistency spreadable straight from the fridge, not runny
- [x] Kept refrigerated, used within the week

## Out of scope

- An egg-free aquafaba version — noted as a future variant, not built yet.
