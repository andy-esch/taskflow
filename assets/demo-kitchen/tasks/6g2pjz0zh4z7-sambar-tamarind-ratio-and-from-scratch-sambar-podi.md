# Sambar: tamarind ratio and from-scratch sambar podi

## Objective

Toast and grind a sambar podi (coriander, chana dal, urad dal, dried red
chile, fenugreek, curry leaf) instead of buying the tin, then balance it
against tamarind pulp and jaggery until the sourness reads bright rather than
puckering. Start with a 2:1 tamarind-water soak strained for pulp, and add
jaggery in small increments — the sour-sweet balance is the whole dish, and
it's easy to blow past the point where they cancel out into flatness.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Sambar podi toasted and ground fresh, not substituted from a jar
- [ ] Tamarind pulp extracted at a working 2:1 water ratio, strained of seeds/fiber
- [ ] Jaggery added incrementally with tasting between additions
- [ ] Vegetables (drumstick, okra, or pearl onion) hold shape, not mush

## Out of scope

- Idli/dosa pairing and the fermented batter side of the meal.
