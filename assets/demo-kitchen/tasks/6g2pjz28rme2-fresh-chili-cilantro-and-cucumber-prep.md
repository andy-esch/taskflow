# Fresh chili, cilantro, and cucumber prep

## Objective

Standardize the fresh-vegetable layer so every sandwich gets the same bite:
Thai bird's-eye chilies sliced into thin rounds (seeds left in for real
heat), cilantro left as whole sprigs rather than chopped so it reads as a
distinct layer, and English cucumber cut into long thin spears rather than
rounds so they don't slide out when the sandwich is bitten into.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Chili slicing thickness settled and consistent
- [ ] Cilantro kept as sprigs, not minced
- [ ] Cucumber spear length and thickness fixed for the roll size
- [ ] Layer order tested for what stays put under a bite

## Out of scope

- Growing cilantro or chilies at home — store-bought for now.
