# Panch phoron tempering for a Bengali-style dal

## Objective

Try the five-spice Bengali temper — equal parts fenugreek, nigella, fennel,
cumin, and mustard seed, bloomed whole in mustard oil until the mustard oil
loses its raw sharpness — on a plain masoor dal instead of the usual
South Indian tadka. Fenugreek burns bitter fast, so it needs to go in with
(not before) the rest of the mix, not staged first like mustard seed in a
standard tadka.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Panch phoron mixed in equal parts and bloomed together, not staged
- [ ] Mustard oil brought past its raw pungency before the spices go in
- [ ] Fenugreek doesn't scorch bitter
- [ ] Side-by-side taste against the standard tadka version

## Out of scope

- Sourcing an actual Bengali five-spice blend pre-mixed — grinding our own ratio.
