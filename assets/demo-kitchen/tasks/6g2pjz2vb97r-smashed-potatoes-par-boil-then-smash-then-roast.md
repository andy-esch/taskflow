# Smashed potatoes: par-boil then smash then roast

## Objective

Par-boil small waxy-ish potatoes in well-salted water until a knife slides in
with almost no resistance (past the point normally used for whole roasted
potatoes), then smash flat with the bottom of a glass to maximize craggy
surface area for crisping. Too little par-boil and the smash tears rather
than flattens; too much and they fall apart before they hit the sheet pan.
Roast hot — 450°F — on well-oiled parchment so the ragged edges color before
the centers dry out.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Par-boil doneness dialed to "knife slides in with almost no resistance"
- [x] Smash thickness settled — flat enough to crisp, intact enough to lift
- [x] Roast temp confirmed at 450°F for max edge crisping
- [x] Edges audibly crisp, centers still creamy

## Out of scope

- Air-fryer version — oven roasting only for this pass.
