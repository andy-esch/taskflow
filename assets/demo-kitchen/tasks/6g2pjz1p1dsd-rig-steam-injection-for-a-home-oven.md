# Rig steam injection for a home oven

## Objective

A home oven can't match a deck oven's built-in steam injection, so improvise
it: preheat a heavy cast-iron pan on the bottom rack, and at load time pour
in a cup of ice cubes (they melt slower than water, giving a longer steam
burst) and shut the door immediately. The steam window matters — it needs to
hold for the first 8-10 minutes of the bake, before the crust sets, or the
rice-flour crust won't get the chance to gelatinize and crisp properly.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Cast-iron pan preheated with the oven, not added cold
- [ ] Ice-cube steam burst timed and holds visible fog for 8+ minutes
- [ ] Oven door opened only once, at load, to keep steam in
- [ ] Crust result compared against a no-steam control bake

## Out of scope

- A dedicated steam oven or spray-bottle misting technique.
