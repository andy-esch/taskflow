# Hasselback potatoes: slicing jig and fanning

## Objective

Rest each potato in a pair of wooden chopsticks laid alongside it so the
knife stops before cutting all the way through — that's the whole trick to
the fan without slicing clean segments off. Cuts at roughly 3mm intervals
give the best balance of crisp-edge-to-tender-center ratio; much thinner and
the slices dry out before the center cooks, much thicker and it stops
reading as "hasselback." Basting with butter partway through the bake (once
the slices have relaxed enough to fan open) gets butter down between the cuts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Chopstick jig confirmed to stop the knife reliably before full cutthrough
- [ ] Slice interval settled around 3mm
- [ ] Fanning happens naturally in the oven without forcing the slices apart
- [ ] Mid-bake baste timed to when slices have already started to fan

## Out of scope

- Stuffing the slices with cheese or herbs — plain version first.
