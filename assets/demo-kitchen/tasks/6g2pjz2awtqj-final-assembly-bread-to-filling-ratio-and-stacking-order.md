# Final assembly: bread-to-filling ratio and stacking order

## Objective

Fix the layering order and rough per-sandwich gram weights so the roll holds
together through the meal instead of blowing out on the second bite: mayo
and pate on the cut faces first (they act as a moisture barrier for the
bread), then the tofu, then do chua and cucumber, then herbs and chili on
top so they're the first thing tasted. Too much do chua and the roll gets
soggy in ten minutes; too little and the pickle doesn't register at all.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Layering order fixed: spreads on bread first, wet pickle components last
- [ ] Per-sandwich gram targets set for filling, pickle, and herbs
- [ ] Roll holds together through a full sandwich, no structural collapse
- [ ] Sogginess check at the 10-minute mark — bread still has crackle

## Out of scope

- Wrapping/packaging for takeaway — eaten fresh at the table for now.
