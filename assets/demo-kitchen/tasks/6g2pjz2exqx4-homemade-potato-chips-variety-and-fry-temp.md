# Homemade potato chips: variety and fry temp

## Objective

Low-starch, waxy potatoes (Yukon Gold works, a true waxy like a fingerling
works better) hold together better than a starchy russet when sliced thin.
Rinse slices in cold water until it runs clear to strip surface starch, then
fry in two stages: a lower first pass around 300°F to cook through, rest to
let residual heat even out, then a second flash at 350-375°F to crisp and
color. Skipping the two-stage fry gives chips that are either pale and
floppy or dark on the edge and raw in the middle.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Variety settled on after a side-by-side slice-and-fry test
- [x] Rinse step run until water runs clear before frying
- [x] Two-stage fry temps confirmed (~300°F then ~350-375°F)
- [x] Chips crisp all the way through, even color, no raw centers

## Out of scope

- Kettle-style (double-fried in the same oil) technique — separate variant.
