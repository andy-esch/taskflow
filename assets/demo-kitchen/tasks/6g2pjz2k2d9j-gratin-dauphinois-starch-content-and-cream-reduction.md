# Gratin dauphinois: starch content and cream reduction

## Objective

Traditional dauphinois sets from starch and reduced cream alone — no flour,
no cheese as a binder. Waxy potatoes don't release enough starch to thicken
the cream and the gratin stays soupy; a starchier all-purpose potato (Yukon
Gold, not a full russet which turns grainy) releases just enough. Simmer the
cream with garlic and nutmeg for 5 minutes before layering to pre-thicken it
slightly, then bake at 325°F low and slow (75-90 minutes) so the potatoes
cook through before the top overbrowns.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Potato variety confirmed to release enough starch to set the cream
- [ ] Cream pre-simmered with garlic and nutmeg before layering
- [ ] Bake temp held at 325°F for even cook-through
- [ ] Sets firm enough to hold a clean slice, no flour or cheese used

## Out of scope

- The cheese-topped "gratin" variant — that's a different dish by this
  household's own naming convention.
