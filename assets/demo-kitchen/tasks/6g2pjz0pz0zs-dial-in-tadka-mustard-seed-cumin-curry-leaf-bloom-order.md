# Dial in tadka: mustard seed, cumin, curry leaf bloom order

## Objective

The tempering order matters more than the ingredient list. Get ghee to
shimmering (not smoking) over medium heat, drop mustard seeds first and wait
for them to pop, then cumin seeds until they darken a shade, then curry
leaves last — they spatter hard and finish in under 10 seconds. A tadka
added too early tastes raw; one that scorches turns bitter and there's no
saving the pot.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Mustard seeds pop fully before cumin goes in
- [x] Curry leaves added last, off heat within 10 seconds of hitting the oil
- [x] No scorched (black, not golden) spices across three test batches
- [x] Tadka poured hot over finished dal, not stirred in and reheated

## Out of scope

- Panch phoron and other regional temper blends — tracked separately.
