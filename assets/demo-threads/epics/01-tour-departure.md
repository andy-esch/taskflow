# Tour departure

**Goal.** Prepare the steel touring bike for a fully loaded departure.

## Why this is its own epic

The departure date turns otherwise independent workshop jobs into one release decision. The bike
does not leave until its chassis, drivetrain, wheels, loadout, and loaded handling converge.

## Out of scope

- Route planning and camping-kit selection.
- Cosmetic restoration that does not affect the departure check.
