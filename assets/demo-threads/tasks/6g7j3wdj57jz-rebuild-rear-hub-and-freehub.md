# Rebuild rear hub and freehub

## Objective

Strip the rear hub after a wet season, replace rough bearings, service the freehub, and set preload
before the wheel receives its final dish and tension.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Hub shell, axle, and freehub inspected
- [x] Bearings and pawl springs renewed
- [x] Preload set with free rotation and no lateral play

## Out of scope

- Final wheel dish and spoke tension.
