# Run a loaded shakedown ride

## Objective

Ride the finished bike over climbing, braking, and rough-road sections at departure weight, then
resolve every mechanical issue before approving it for the tour.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Forty-kilometre mixed-surface route completed at departure weight
- [ ] Brakes, shifting, wheels, and load retention rechecked hot
- [ ] No unresolved mechanical blocker remains

## Out of scope

- Fitness testing or route reconnaissance.
