# Inspect touring frame and fork

## Objective

Clean the steel frame and fork, inspect every braze-on and high-stress junction, then verify fork
alignment before service work commits the bike to a loaded departure.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Frame and fork cleaned for inspection
- [x] Welds, dropouts, and rack mounts checked under bright light
- [x] Fork alignment and headset seats verified

## Out of scope

- Cosmetic chips that do not expose corrosion or cracking.
