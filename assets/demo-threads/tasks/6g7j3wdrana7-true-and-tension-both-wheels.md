# True and tension both wheels

## Objective

Bring both wheels into dish, remove lateral and radial runout, and balance spoke tension after the
rear-hub rebuild so they are ready for loaded mileage.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both wheels centered and dished in the frame
- [ ] Lateral and radial runout within workshop tolerance
- [ ] Spoke tension balanced and stress-relieved

## Out of scope

- Replacing rims unless inspection finds structural damage.
