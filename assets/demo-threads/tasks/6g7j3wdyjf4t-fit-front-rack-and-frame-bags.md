# Fit front rack and frame bags

## Objective

Mount the low-rider rack and frame bags, then check cable, steering, heel, and tire clearance with
the bike carrying the actual departure load.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Crown mount installed without stressing the fork
- [ ] Rack and bags secured to torque specification
- [ ] Steering and rider clearances checked under load

## Out of scope

- Choosing or packing the camping kit itself.
