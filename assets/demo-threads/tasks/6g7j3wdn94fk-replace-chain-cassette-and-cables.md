# Replace chain cassette and cables

## Objective

Replace the worn chain and cassette together, renew both control cables, and establish clean baseline
shifting and braking before the loaded road test.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Chain wear and cassette hooking documented
- [x] Chain, cassette, and cables replaced
- [x] Shifting and braking indexed on the stand

## Out of scope

- Gearing changes beyond the proven touring range.
